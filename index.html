How to order

JRM Store uses Discord tickets for order assistance and delivery coordination.

01 / SELECT

Choose your menu

Open a product, compare the available durations and choose the license you want.

02 / OPEN TICKET

Contact us on Discord

Use the TicketKing ticket panel in our Discord server and provide your product and selected license.

03 / DELIVERY

Order assistance & delivery

Our team reviews the ticket and coordinates the order according to the product's delivery instructions.
